Every grant outcome in this dashboard has been matched against two resident-driven plans for Kashmere Gardens. Under the Complete Community Action Plan, each outcome is matched to the goal it most directly advances and to the one specific action item within that goal it most directly serves. Under the 2026 Kashmere Gardens Priorities, each outcome is matched to the priority action it most directly advances and, where one fits, to the specific key action within it. So you can see which parts of each plan current grant funding is, and isn't, reaching.

Counts use each outcome's primary match (the one plan item it most directly advances) unless a view says otherwise; many outcomes also have up to two secondary matches. Colors are consistent throughout:

Complete Community Plan vs. 2026 Priorities

Side-by-side summary, a crosswalk of where outcomes land under each plan, and the main differences in how grant work lines up.

The two plans are organized differently. The Complete Community Plan nests 82 action items under its goals. The 2026 plan adds a priority-action layer between its goals and its 90 key actions. Outcomes that advance a 2026 priority action broadly, without fitting one key action, count toward the priority action but not toward any key action.
